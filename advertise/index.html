Advertise with CruisE

Reach Canadian drivers at the moment they think about their car — fuelling up, planning a trip or checking what service is due.

Who you reach

  • Car owners and lessees across Canada, in English and French.
  • People who actively track fuel, maintenance, tires and lease kilometres.
  • Targeting by province, language and season — e.g. winter tires in Quebec from October.

The placement

A banner on the CruisE home screen (My Vehicle), shown between the driver’s vehicles and Roadside Assistance, clearly labelled “Sponsored”. Tapping it opens your page inside the app.

Good fits

Tire shops and installers, garages and service centres, car washes and detailing, EV charging, insurance brokers, dealers, parts and accessories retailers, driving schools.

Our standards

Ads must be honest, legal in Canada and relevant to drivers. We don’t accept ads for alcohol, cannabis, gambling or anything that encourages unsafe driving. Advertisers never receive users’ personal information.

Rates and availability

Rates depend on reach, targeting and duration. Write to us with your goals, province(s) and dates, and we’ll send a proposal.
