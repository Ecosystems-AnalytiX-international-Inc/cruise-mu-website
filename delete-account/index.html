Account & Data Deletion

Ecosystem & AnalytiX International Ltd. · CruisE (Canada)

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your CruisE account and all associated personal and vehicle data. We provide two easy methods: directly within the mobile application or using the public web request form below without needing to install the app or sign in.

Privacy Officer: Ecosystem & AnalytiX International Ltd. — privacy@cruise-mu.com
Processing Commitment: In-app deletion is instantaneous. Web-initiated deletion requests are verified and processed within 30 days pursuant to Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25).

Method 1: Delete Directly in the Mobile App

In-App Self-Service Deletion Instant Removal

If you have the CruisE app installed on your iPhone or Android device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the CruisE application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the Security & Support section and tap Delete account.
  4. Review the warning prompt and confirm by tapping Delete account.

Your session will be terminated immediately, and all your personal and vehicle data will be purged from our production databases.

What Data is Deleted and What is Retained

Category Data Types Action & Policy
Account & Identity Name, email address, phone number, encrypted password hash, device identifiers. Permanently Deleted from active databases upon account deletion.
Vehicles & Records Vehicle details (make, model, year, plate), fill-up & charging history, expense receipts, maintenance logs, reminders, tire sets. Permanently Deleted. All receipts and logged metrics are purged.
AI & Consents Ask CruisE question summaries, user consent history, paywall view logs. Permanently Deleted.
Financial Records (Exception) Anonymized purchase transaction IDs for Apple App Store / Google Play subscription ledger verification. Retained Anonymously without user linkage solely as required by Canadian tax laws (e.g. Canada Revenue Agency 6-year retention rule).
System Backups (Exception) Encrypted automated operational backup archives. Overwritten within 30 days in the regular rotational cycle.

Subscriptions and App Stores

Please note that deleting your CruisE account does not automatically cancel active paid subscriptions billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid unwanted charges by Apple or Google:

  • iOS users: Open iPhone Settings › [Your Name] › Subscriptions › CruisE › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android users: Open Google Play Store › Profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › CruisE › Cancel.
