Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request complete deletion of your CruisE account and associated vehicle telemetry under Canadian PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25.

In-App Instant Removal Web Requests 30-Day Processing Privacy Officer admin@cruise-mu.com
Compliance Commitment: In-app deletion is executed instantaneously. Web-initiated deletion requests are verified via email and completely processed within 30 days pursuant to Canadian privacy standards.

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Instant Removal

If you have CruisE installed on your iPhone or Android device, you can delete your account immediately without waiting for email verification:

  1. Open the CruisE app on your device.
  2. Tap the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the Security & Support section and tap Delete account.
  4. Review the permanent deletion warning and confirm by tapping Delete account.

Your session will be terminated immediately and all personal records purged from production databases.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

CategoryData TypesAction & Policy
Account & Identity Name, email address, phone number, encrypted password hash, device identifiers. Permanently Deleted from active databases upon account deletion.
Vehicles & Records Vehicle details (make, model, year, plate), fill-up & charging history, expense receipts, maintenance logs, reminders, tire sets. Permanently Deleted. All receipts and logged metrics are purged.
Financial Records (Exception) Anonymized purchase transaction IDs for Apple App Store / Google Play subscription ledger verification. Retained Anonymously solely as required by Canadian tax laws (e.g. Canada Revenue Agency 6-year rule).
System Backups (Exception) Encrypted automated operational backup archives. Overwritten within 30 days in the regular rotational cycle.
Important Subscription Notice: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions billed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To avoid unwanted charges, cancel the subscription directly in your Apple ID or Google Play Subscriptions menu.
