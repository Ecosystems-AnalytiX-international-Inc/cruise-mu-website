CruisE - My Vehicle Partner Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc.

1. Who we are

CruisE - My Vehicle Partner is a mobile application and related website that help drivers record, understand and manage vehicle ownership and running costs. The service is operated by Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc., an Ontario corporation, with its registered office at William Carson Crescent, Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("CruisE", "we", "us" or "our"). Our contact details are set out in section 16.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, retain and protect personal information through the CruisE mobile application, www.cruise-mu.com, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.ca, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.com and related customer-support channels. It is intended for users of the Canada, United States, United Kingdom and Australia editions. It applies to personal information processed in connection with those services, including where a user accesses the service while travelling. Different legal rights may apply depending on where you live. We do not represent that every feature, data source or legal reminder is available in every country.

Privacy responsibility. Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc. is responsible for the personal information it collects and controls. For purposes of the UK GDPR, the company acts as the data controller where that law applies. Our Privacy Officer is responsible for privacy inquiries and can be contacted at admin@cruise-mu.com.

The Privacy Officer’s identity, title and contact details are available on the website and in the app. If a different person or entity is appointed as UK representative, the representative’s mandate and contact details shall be kept current.

Our internal privacy governance procedures describe how we assign responsibilities, assess privacy risks, manage suppliers, handle requests and complaints, retain and destroy information, and respond to security incidents.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number (if provided), account identifier, country, province or state, language, verification status and password credentials. Passwords are stored using a one-way password hash; we do not store them in readable form. Authentication tokens may be stored securely on your device.
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, trim or version, nickname, odometer readings and other vehicle attributes you enter or select. You may optionally provide a vehicle identification number (VIN) and, where the UK registration lookup is enabled, a vehicle registration mark (number plate). A VIN or registration mark may be personal information when it can be linked to you or another identifiable person.
  • Vehicle records you create: fuel fill-ups and electric-vehicle charging sessions (including date, quantity, price and odometer reading), expenses, maintenance and repair records, tyre or tire sets, reminders, and related notes.
  • Receipt and supporting images: images that you choose to upload for a fill-up, service or expense. Images may incidentally contain personal information, such as names, addresses, payment references or information about other individuals. Please crop or redact information that is not needed for the record.
  • Finance and lease information you choose to enter: agreement type (including lease, personal contract purchase (PCP), contract hire, loan or novated lease), commencement date, term, mileage or distance allowance, payments, interest rate, final or residual payment, lender or finance-provider name, and related figures. We do not access your lender account or obtain your credit file through these fields.
  • Mileage-logbook entries: date, distance, purpose of trip, odometer readings and any start or end locations you choose to type in. We do not create a continuous or automatic journey history through the logbook.
  • Ask CruisE information: preset questions you select and, where enabled by your plan, free-text questions you submit, together with the vehicle context described in section 4.
  • Privacy and service choices: consent and preference records, including the choice made, date and time, source, relevant notice or policy version, and any later withdrawal or change.
  • Communications: messages, requests, feedback and attachments that you send to our support or privacy contact addresses.

Information created when you use CruisE

  • Subscription information: plan, trial status, purchase and renewal status, start and expiry dates, and transaction or entitlement identifiers received from Apple or Google, including through RevenueCat. Apple or Google process payments through their systems. We do not receive full payment-card or bank-account details from the app stores as part of the subscription-status feed; the stores may process payment details under their own terms and privacy notices.
  • Service-event information: limited events needed to operate the service, such as whether a Premium offer was displayed or dismissed and the number of Ask CruisE answers used for plan limits. These events are also used to prevent abuse and manage the frequency of in-app offers.
  • Technical and security information: device type, operating system, app version, browser or device details where relevant, IP address, date and time of a request, server logs, diagnostic and error information that our systems or service providers generate, and security events used for troubleshooting, fraud prevention and service integrity.

Location

Device location is accessed only when you actively choose a feature that needs it, such as "Start from my location" in the trip planner or a nearby fuel-price search, and only to the extent needed for that request. Depending on the feature, location or route information may be sent to our server and/or directly to Google Maps Platform. We do not intentionally track your location in the background or maintain a continuous location history. The logbook records only information you enter. For Roadside Assistance, if you choose to share your live or current location, your device may send it through its operating-system sharing function to the person or service you select; that sharing is governed by the device and recipient services as well as this Policy. Location permissions can be changed in your device settings.

Just-in-time notices: Where a feature requests access to device location, camera, photo library or other device permissions, the app explains the purpose at the point of collection and requests the permission required by the operating system.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for payment-card numbers, bank-account login credentials, contact lists, background location, health information or biometric identifiers as part of the ordinary CruisE service. However, a receipt, support message or free-text question could contain information you choose to include. Please do not upload or enter sensitive or third-party information unless it is necessary and you are entitled to provide it.

3. Why we use it

We use personal information for the purposes below and only where we have an applicable legal basis or other permission under the law that applies:

  • to provide, personalise and maintain the features you request, including your garage, consumption comparisons, reminders, maintenance records, finance and lease tracking, mileage logbook and exports, trip estimates, insights, maps and Roadside Assistance;
  • to create, authenticate and secure your account, verify your email address, provide customer support and communicate essential service information;
  • to administer subscriptions, trials, plan entitlements, renewals, cancellations and service notices, including notices required by applicable consumer law or app-store rules;
  • to process Ask CruisE requests and return vehicle-related information or suggestions;
  • to secure the service, detect and prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorised access and technical faults, and maintain, test and improve reliability;
  • to meet legal, regulatory, accounting, tax and law-enforcement obligations, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

For users in the United Kingdom, our legal bases are described in section 15. For other locations, we process information where permitted by applicable privacy law, including to provide a service you request, with your consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, or for legitimate interests that are not overridden by your rights. Where processing is optional and based on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

Optional marketing. We may send you occasional emails about CruisE features, vehicle-running tips, promotions or partner offers only where we have obtained a valid permission or consent required by the law applicable to you. Marketing is off by default and is not a condition of using CruisE. You can change your preference in Account › Privacy or use the unsubscribe mechanism in each marketing message. We identify ourselves and provide contact details and a working unsubscribe method. We do not give your email address or other contact details to a partner so that the partner can market to you. Our email programme complies with Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL), UK PECR and the Australian Spam Act, as applicable.

Mileage logbook and tax records. The logbook and its exports are record-keeping tools that may help you prepare records for a tax authority, such as the CRA, IRS, HMRC or ATO. We do not automatically send your logbook to a tax authority. You decide whether to export or submit it and remain responsible for checking the applicable record-keeping requirements.

No sale or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not disclose it for cross-context behavioural advertising or targeted advertising as those concepts are defined by applicable privacy laws. We do not knowingly track you across unrelated apps or websites. We do disclose information to service providers to operate features, as described in section 7.

Automated processing. CruisE currently uses calculations and automated tools to generate consumption comparisons, reminders, trip estimates, lease and finance projections, and Ask CruisE suggestions. These outputs are informational and are not used by us to make decisions about a person’s eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing, essential services or other decisions that significantly affect that person’s rights or interests.

4. Ask CruisE (artificial intelligence)

Ask CruisE is an optional feature that uses an artificial-intelligence model supplied by Anthropic to generate general vehicle-related information. When you submit a request, our server sends Anthropic:

  • the question or prompt you submit, which may include personal information if you type it into the prompt; and
  • a vehicle-context summary for the vehicle you selected, which may include make, model, year, province or state, odometer readings, recent consumption and cost information, maintenance history, and lease or finance figures. We treat this contextual summary as personal information where it can be linked to your account or vehicle.

Privacy: We do not intentionally attach your account name, email address, mobile number, VIN or UK number plate to the vehicle-context summary sent to Anthropic. However, if you type identifying or sensitive information into your question, that information may be transmitted with the prompt. Do not enter names, home or work addresses, registration marks, account details or other information that is not needed to answer a vehicle question.

Please use Ask CruisE only for vehicle-related questions. Do not use it for emergency decisions, legal, tax, insurance, credit or financial advice, or as a substitute for a qualified mechanic or vehicle manufacturer instructions.

We count Ask CruisE requests to administer plan limits. We do not retain the full prompts or responses in our application database after the request is completed. AI-generated answers may be inaccurate or incomplete.

5. Advertising

Some screens may display banners clearly marked "Sponsored". The app selects the banner locally using the country, province or state, language and plan settings available to the app. Those selection inputs are not sent to the advertiser by CruisE for that purpose. Banner images are hosted in a content library on GitHub; GitHub may receive your IP address and technical request data when your device downloads an image. If you tap a banner, your device opens the advertiser’s page. The advertiser or its providers may then collect information such as your IP address, device or browser data, cookies or other identifiers under their own privacy notice. We do not provide advertisers with your CruisE account records or contact details.

6. Identifying your vehicle and reference data

  • Optional VIN lookup. If you enter a VIN and request a lookup, our server sends the VIN to the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration’s vPIC vehicle decoder to retrieve vehicle specifications such as make, model, year and engine. We do not intentionally send your name, email address or account profile with the VIN. NHTSA processes the request under its own systems and applicable notices.
  • UK vehicle-registration lookup. Where enabled, if you enter a UK vehicle registration mark and request a lookup, our server sends that registration mark to the UK Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency’s Vehicle Enquiry Service to retrieve available vehicle details, such as make, fuel type, engine size, vehicle tax status and MOT information. This lookup is subject to DVLA service availability, eligibility, access conditions and permitted-use requirements.
  • Official vehicle-consumption data and fuel prices. We maintain copies or extracts of public or licensed datasets from Natural Resources Canada, the US Environmental Protection Agency, the UK Vehicle Certification Agency and the Australian Government’s Green Vehicle Guide. A lookup against a copy held on our own server does not itself send your account details to those agencies. Where a fuel-price or nearby-location provider is used in the UK or Australia, it may receive a general area, search query, device or network data needed to return results.

8. Where your information is stored

The primary CruisE application database and receipt-photo storage are hosted in Canada in the AWS Montreal region. Some providers listed in section 7 process information in the United States and other countries. As a result, information may be accessed or processed outside the country where you live, including by the provider’s personnel or authorities under the laws of that country.

Before transferring personal information outside a jurisdiction, we assess the applicable legal requirements and the safeguards available. For Quebec personal information, this includes the privacy impact assessment required under Quebec Law 25, together with written agreements. For UK transfers, we use applicable UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or Standard Contractual Clauses with transfer risk assessments.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on the laws that apply where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • to request access to personal information we hold about you and, where required, a copy or export in a commonly used electronic format;
  • to request correction of personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • to request deletion or erasure, subject to legal exceptions and our need to retain certain records;
  • to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • to object to, or request restriction of, certain processing, and to request data portability where the law provides those rights;
  • to change or withdraw device-location permission through the app or your device settings; and
  • to complain to us and, where applicable, to the privacy regulator in your jurisdiction.

To make a privacy request, email admin@cruise-mu.com or use the in-app privacy tools, where available. We respond within the statutory time limit (typically 30 days or one month) without fee unless permitted by law.

11. Delete your account

In the app, go to Account › Delete my account and complete the confirmation step. Alternatively, submit the deletion form at www.cruise-mu.com/delete-account/ or email admin@cruise-mu.com from the email address associated with the account, with the subject "Delete my account".

Deleting your CruisE account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel the subscription separately through the applicable store settings to prevent future renewals.

After we verify a deletion request, we delete or de-identify the account, vehicle records, receipt photos, finance and lease details and logbook from active production systems within 30 days. Backups are overwritten on a rolling cycle and clear within a further 30 days.

12. Security

We protect personal information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity:

  • data sent between your device and our servers is protected using HTTPS/TLS in transit;
  • passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage where supported by the operating system;
  • receipt photos are stored in private storage and are made available through access-controlled links that expire;
  • access to personal information is limited to personnel and contracted service providers who need access to operate, support or secure CruisE and who are subject to confidentiality and access controls; and
  • we maintain processes for recording, assessing, escalating and responding to security incidents.

13. Children

CruisE is designed for adult vehicle owners, lessees and drivers. The proposed minimum account age is 18. We do not knowingly permit a person under 18 to create an account. If we learn that an account was created by someone under 18, we will take steps to suspend or delete it and associated personal information.

14. The websites

The websites www.cruise-mu.com, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.ca, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.com are intended not to use non-essential advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider, GitHub, may collect technical information such as IP address, browser or device details and request logs to deliver and secure the site.
