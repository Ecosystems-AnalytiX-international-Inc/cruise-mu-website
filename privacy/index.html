1. Who we are

CruisE - My Vehicle Partner is a mobile application and related website that help drivers record, understand and manage vehicle ownership and running costs. The service is operated by Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc., an Ontario corporation, with its registered office at William Carson Crescent, Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("CruisE", "we", "us" or "our"). Our contact details are set out in section 16.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, retain and protect personal information through the CruisE mobile application, www.cruise-mu.com, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.ca, www.cruise-myvehiclepartner.com and related customer-support channels. It is intended for users of the Canada, United States, United Kingdom and Australia editions. It applies to personal information processed in connection with those services, including where a user accesses the service while travelling. Different legal rights may apply depending on where you live. We do not represent that every feature, data source or legal reminder is available in every country.

Privacy Officer: Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc. is responsible for personal information under its control. For UK GDPR purposes, the company acts as data controller. Contact our Privacy Officer at admin@cruise-mu.com.

Our internal privacy governance procedures describe how we assign responsibilities, assess privacy risks, manage suppliers, handle requests and complaints, retain and destroy information, and respond to security incidents.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number (if provided), account identifier, country, province or state, language, verification status and password credentials. Passwords are stored using a one-way password hash; we do not store them in readable form. Authentication tokens may be stored securely on your device.
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, trim or version, nickname, odometer readings and other vehicle attributes you enter or select. You may optionally provide a vehicle identification number (VIN) and, where the UK registration lookup is enabled, a vehicle registration mark (number plate). A VIN or registration mark may be personal information when it can be linked to you or another identifiable person.
  • Vehicle records you create: fuel fill-ups and electric-vehicle charging sessions (including date, quantity, price and odometer reading), expenses, maintenance and repair records, tyre or tire sets, reminders, and related notes.
  • Receipt and supporting images: images that you choose to upload for a fill-up, service or expense. Images may incidentally contain personal information, such as names, addresses, payment references or information about other individuals. Please crop or redact information that is not needed for the record.
  • Finance and lease information you choose to enter: agreement type (including lease, personal contract purchase (PCP), contract hire, loan or novated lease), commencement date, term, mileage or distance allowance, payments, interest rate, final or residual payment, lender or finance-provider name, and related figures. We do not access your lender account or obtain your credit file through these fields.
  • Mileage-logbook entries: date, distance, purpose of trip, odometer readings and any start or end locations you choose to type in. We do not create a continuous or automatic journey history through the logbook.
  • Ask CruisE information: preset questions you select and, where enabled by your plan, free-text questions you submit, together with the vehicle context described in section 4.
  • Privacy and service choices: consent and preference records, including the choice made, date and time, source, relevant notice or policy version, and any later withdrawal or change.
  • Communications: messages, requests, feedback and attachments that you send to our support or privacy contact addresses.

Information created when you use CruisE

  • Subscription information: plan, trial status, purchase and renewal status, start and expiry dates, and transaction or entitlement identifiers received from Apple or Google, including through RevenueCat. Apple or Google process payments through their systems. We do not receive full payment-card or bank-account details from the app stores as part of the subscription-status feed.
  • Service-event information: limited events needed to operate the service, such as whether a Premium offer was displayed or dismissed and the number of Ask CruisE answers used for plan limits.
  • Technical and security information: device type, operating system, app version, browser details, IP address, date and time of requests, server logs, diagnostic and error information for troubleshooting, fraud prevention and service integrity.

Location Data

Device location is accessed only when you actively choose a feature that needs it, such as "Start from my location" in the trip planner or a nearby fuel-price search, and only to the extent needed for that request. We do not track your location in the background or maintain a continuous location history. Location permissions can be modified at any time in your device settings.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for payment-card numbers, bank-account credentials, contact lists, background continuous location, health information, or biometric identifiers as part of the ordinary CruisE service.

3. Why we use it

We use personal information for the following specific purposes:

  • to provide, personalise and maintain the features you request (garage, consumption comparisons, reminders, maintenance records, finance and lease tracking, mileage logbook, trip estimates, insights and Roadside Assistance);
  • to create, authenticate and secure your account, verify your email address, provide customer support and communicate service information;
  • to administer subscriptions, trials, renewals, cancellations and mandatory service notices;
  • to process Ask CruisE requests and return vehicle-related information or suggestions;
  • to secure the service, detect and prevent fraud, misuse and technical faults;
  • to meet legal, regulatory, accounting, tax and law-enforcement obligations.
No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not disclose it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not track you across unrelated apps or websites.

4. Ask CruisE (artificial intelligence)

Ask CruisE is an optional feature that uses an artificial-intelligence model supplied by Anthropic to generate general vehicle-related information. When you submit a request, our server sends Anthropic:

  • the question or prompt you submit; and
  • a vehicle-context summary (make, model, year, province/state, odometer readings, recent consumption, and maintenance history).

Privacy protection: We do not attach your account name, email address, mobile number, VIN, or number plate to the vehicle-context summary sent to Anthropic. We count Ask CruisE requests to administer plan limits. We do not retain the full prompts or responses in our application database after the request is completed.

5. Advertising

Some screens may display banners clearly marked "Sponsored". The app selects the banner locally using country, province/state, language, and plan settings. Those selection inputs are not sent to the advertiser. We do not provide advertisers with your CruisE account records or contact details.

6. Identifying your vehicle and reference data

  • Optional VIN lookup: Sent to the US NHTSA vPIC vehicle decoder to retrieve specifications. No personal profile is attached.
  • UK vehicle-registration lookup: Sent to the UK DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service to retrieve vehicle details. Subject to DVLA terms.
  • Official vehicle consumption datasets: Natural Resources Canada (NRCan), US EPA, UK VCA, and Australian Green Vehicle Guide figures are stored or referenced on our servers.

8. Where your information is stored

The primary CruisE application database and receipt-photo storage are hosted in Canada (AWS Montreal region). When information is transferred outside Canada, we evaluate legal safeguards including the Privacy Impact Assessment under Quebec Law 25 and Standard Contractual Clauses for the UK and international jurisdictions.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may exercise the following rights:

  • Access, review, and export your personal information in a readable format;
  • Correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete vehicle or personal data;
  • Request deletion (erasure) of your account and vehicle telemetry;
  • Withdraw previously given consent at any time without penalty;
  • Modify device permissions (location, camera, notifications) via device settings.

To exercise any privacy right, email admin@cruise-mu.com or use the in-app controls. Requests are handled within 30 days without charge.

11. Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time through either of two convenient methods:

  1. Inside the Mobile App: Open CruisE › Account › Delete my account.
  2. Via Web Form: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page without installing the app.
Subscription Reminder: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed through Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel your subscription in your device's Apple ID or Google Play Subscriptions menu.

12. Security

CruisE implements industry-standard technical, organizational, and physical safeguards:

  • All network traffic is encrypted using HTTPS and modern TLS 1.3 in transit;
  • Passwords are irreversibly hashed using modern cryptographic algorithms;
  • Receipt images are stored in secure private buckets with expiring pre-signed URLs;
  • Strict role-based access controls and principle of least privilege for engineering staff.

13. Children

CruisE is designed exclusively for drivers and vehicle owners aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly permit minors under 18 to create accounts or collect their information.

14. The websites

Our websites (cruise-mu.com, cruise-myvehiclepartner.ca, cruise-myvehiclepartner.com) do not use invasive third-party tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) collects essential request logs for security and reliability.

15. Information for your country

Canada

Handled pursuant to PIPEDA and provincial laws (Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA). Quebec users enjoy enhanced access, data portability, and de-indexing rights overseen by our Privacy Officer.

United States

We do not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising under the CCPA/CPRA. State privacy requests can be directed to admin@cruise-mu.com.

United Kingdom

Processed under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. The supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Australia

Handled in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles. You may contact the OAIC at oaic.gov.au.
