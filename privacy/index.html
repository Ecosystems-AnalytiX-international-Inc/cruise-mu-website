Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective 11 November 2026 · Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc. (Ontario, Canada)

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, retain and protect personal information through the CruisE mobile application, www.cruise-mu.com and related customer-support channels. It is intended for users of the Canada, United States, United Kingdom and Australia editions. Different legal rights may apply depending on where you live.

Data Controller & Privacy Officer: Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc., William Carson Crescent, Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
Privacy Inquiries: adminprivacy@cruise-mu.com | General: info@cruise-mu.com

1. Who we are

CruisE - My Vehicle Partner is a mobile application and related website operated by Ecosystems & AnalytiX International Inc., an Ontario corporation ("CruisE", "we", "us" or "our"). For the purposes of the UK GDPR, the company acts as the data controller where that law applies. Our Privacy Officer is responsible for privacy inquiries and can be contacted at adminprivacy@cruise-mu.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: name, email address, mobile number (if provided), account identifier, country, province or state, language, verification status and password credentials (stored using a secure one-way hash).
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, trim or version, nickname, odometer readings, and vehicle attributes you enter or select. You may optionally provide a vehicle identification number (VIN) or vehicle registration mark (number plate).
  • Vehicle records you create: fuel fill-ups and EV charging sessions (date, quantity, price, odometer), expenses, maintenance/service records, tyre/tire sets, reminders, and notes.
  • Receipt images: photos you choose to upload for records. Please crop or redact unnecessary personal or payment card data.
  • Finance and lease information: agreement type (lease, Personal Contract Purchase / PCP, contract hire, loan, or novated lease), commencement date, term, distance allowance, payments, interest rate, final/residual payment, and lender name. We do not access your bank or lender account.
  • Mileage-logbook entries: date, distance, trip purpose, odometer readings, and start/end locations you enter. CruisE does not create continuous or automatic background journey tracking.
  • Ask CruisE information: preset questions selected or free-text questions submitted, along with anonymous vehicle context.
  • Privacy and service choices: consent records, date/time, and preference choices.
  • Communications: messages and feedback sent to support or privacy contacts.

Information created when you use CruisE

  • Subscription information: plan, trial status, purchase and renewal status, start/expiry dates, and identifiers received from Apple or Google via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store credit card details.
  • Service-event information: operational events (e.g., whether an offer was displayed and Ask CruisE monthly question counters) used to administer plan limits and prevent abuse.
  • Technical and security information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, request timestamps, server logs, and diagnostic data for security and troubleshooting.

Location

Device location is accessed only when you actively choose a feature requiring it (e.g. "Start from my location" in trip planner or nearby fuel searches) and only to the extent needed. We do not track your location in the background. In Roadside Assistance, sharing location is user-initiated through your device's native sharing sheet.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank account login credentials, contact lists, background location, health information, or biometric identifiers.

3. Why we use it

We process personal information under applicable legal bases:

  • To provide, personalise, and maintain features (garage, consumption comparisons, reminders, maintenance, finance/lease tracking, logbook, maps, Roadside Assistance).
  • To authenticate and secure accounts and provide customer support.
  • To administer subscriptions, trials, and plan limits.
  • To process Ask CruisE vehicle inquiries.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse, and security threats.
  • To comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Marketing: Marketing emails are off by default and require affirmative consent. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Ask CruisE (Artificial Intelligence)

Ask CruisE uses an artificial-intelligence model supplied by Anthropic to generate vehicle-related assistance. When you ask a question, our server sends Anthropic:

  • The question or prompt you submit.
  • A vehicle-context summary: make, model, year, province/state, odometer, recent consumption/cost figures, maintenance history, and lease figures.

Privacy protection: We do not attach your name, email, phone, VIN, or registration plate to the vehicle context sent to Anthropic. Prompts and answers are not stored in our database after completion.

5. Advertising

Screens may display banners marked "Sponsored". Banners are selected locally on your device based on country/state and language. No personal profile is transmitted to advertisers. Banner images are served from GitHub. Tapping a sponsor link opens the advertiser's website under their own privacy policy.

6. Identifying your vehicle and reference data

  • Optional VIN lookup: Sent to the US NHTSA vPIC decoder to retrieve specifications. No personal account information is included.
  • UK vehicle registration lookup: Sent to the UK DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service to retrieve vehicle tax and MOT details.
  • Official vehicle consumption data: Matches against extracts of public datasets from Natural Resources Canada (NRCan), the US EPA (FuelEconomy.gov), the UK Vehicle Certification Agency (VCA), and the Australian Green Vehicle Guide (GVG).

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, request data portability, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. Contact our Privacy Officer at adminprivacy@cruise-mu.com. We respond within statutory timelines (30 days or one month) without fee.

11. Delete your account

In the app, navigate to Account › Delete my account. Alternatively, submit the online request at www.cruise-mu.com/delete-account/ or email adminprivacy@cruise-mu.com. Account deletion removes your vehicle records, photos, and history from active systems within 30 days. Note: Deleting your CruisE account does not cancel recurring App Store or Google Play subscriptions; manage your subscription in your device store settings.

12. Security

We protect information with technical and organisational safeguards: TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit, salted one-way password hashing, secure operating-system token storage, and restricted role-based infrastructure access.

13. Children

CruisE is designed for adult vehicle owners and drivers. The minimum account age is 18. We do not knowingly allow individuals under 18 to create accounts.

14. Jurisdiction-Specific Information

  • Canada: Compliant with PIPEDA and Québec Law 25 (Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector). Québec users benefit from rights of access, rectification, portability, and de-indexing.
  • United States: Compliant with applicable state privacy laws (including California CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.
  • United Kingdom: Compliant with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Legal bases include contract performance, legitimate interests, and statutory obligations.
  • Australia: Handled in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).
