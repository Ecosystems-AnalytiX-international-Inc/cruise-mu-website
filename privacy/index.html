Privacy policy

Effective October 2026 · Ecosystem & AnalytiX International Ltd. (Ontario, Canada)

This policy explains what personal information the CruisE app and website collect, why, where it is stored, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. It applies to CruisE in Canada.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Privacy Officer, Ecosystem & AnalytiX International Ltd. — privacy@cruise-mu.com

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: name, email address, mobile number, password (stored encrypted), country, province and language.
  • Vehicles: make, model, year, version, nickname and, if you choose, plate number; odometer readings.
  • Records you enter: fill-ups and charging sessions (date, quantity, price, odometer), expenses, maintenance and services, tire sets, lease details, reminders.
  • Consents: the choices you make for optional uses, with their date and version.
  • Messages you send us by email.

Information created when you use CruisE

  • Subscription status: your plan, whether you are in a free trial, renewal and expiry dates. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details.
  • Paywall and service events: for example that a Premium offer was shown or closed, and counts of Ask CruisE answers (not the questions or answers themselves).
  • Technical data: device type, app version, and server logs (IP address, time, request) kept for security and troubleshooting.

Location

Your location is used only when you choose “Start from my location” in the trip planner. It is sent to our server to calculate the route and is not kept as a location history. Roadside contacts you save stay on your phone. When you share your location from Roadside Assistance, your phone’s own sharing feature sends it to the person you choose.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the app: your garage, consumption and comparisons, reminders, trip estimates, insights and Roadside Assistance.
  • To create and secure your account, including email verification codes.
  • To manage your subscription and send service messages, such as a reminder before a free trial ends.
  • To answer Ask CruisE questions (see section 4).
  • To keep CruisE secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • With your consent only: optional uses described in the app’s Privacy choices.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Advertising

Some screens show sponsored banners, clearly marked “Sponsored”. Banners are images loaded from our own content library. Advertisers do not receive any personal information about you from CruisE. If you tap a banner, the advertiser’s page opens and their own privacy policy applies.

4. Ask CruisE (artificial intelligence)

When you ask a question, our server sends Anthropic (the provider of the Claude AI model) your question and an anonymous summary of the selected vehicle: model, province, odometer, recent consumption and costs, maintenance history and lease pace. We never send your name, email, phone number, plate or VIN. We do not store your questions or the answers; we only count them to apply your plan’s limits. Answers are general information, not professional advice.

6. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on servers outside Canada (currently in South Africa) and may be processed in the United States by the providers above. Before transferring information outside Quebec and Canada, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection and put contractual safeguards in place. Information stored outside Canada may be accessible to authorities of those countries under their laws. We plan to move our hosting to Canada.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal information is deleted from our active systems; copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep limited information longer when the law requires it, for example tax records of purchases held by the app stores.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and copy: export your data from the app (Account › Privacy) or ask us.
  • Correction: edit your information in the app or ask us.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app (Account › Delete account) or submit an online request at cruise-mu.com/delete-account.
  • Consent: change your optional choices at any time in the app.
  • Location: turn location off in the app or in your phone settings.

Write to privacy@cruise-mu.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored encrypted, your sign-in token is kept in your phone’s secure storage, and access to our servers is restricted to authorized people. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident creates a risk of serious injury, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

CruisE is intended for adults who own or drive a vehicle. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

11. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact
