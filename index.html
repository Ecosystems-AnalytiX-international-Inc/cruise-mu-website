Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official "average*" rating?

It is the official combined fuel (or energy) consumption rating for your exact make, model, year, and powertrain trim, published by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan). CruisE calculates your actual consumption from full-tank fill-ups over the past 12 months and compares it to this benchmark.

Does CruisE support electric cars (EVs) and plug-in hybrids?

Yes! You can log charging sessions in kilowatt-hours (kWh) for both home and public chargers, track electricity costs, and compare your consumption with the official government energy ratings (Le/100 km and kWh/100 km).

Do I need to share my GPS location?

No. Location access is optional and only used in the foreground when you choose "Start from my location" in the Trip Cost Planner. CruisE never tracks your location in the background.

How does the 14-day free trial and cancellation work?

You can try all Premium features completely free for 14 days. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. You can cancel anytime in your iPhone or Android subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal, and you won’t be charged.

Is CruisE available in French?

Oui ! The entire app and website are fully bilingual in English and Canadian French. You can switch languages at any time directly in your Account settings.

What makes and models are supported in Canada?

CruisE supports all passenger cars, SUVs, crossover vehicles, and pickup trucks sold in Canada from 1995 to 2026. Every vehicle trim links directly to its official Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) fuel and energy consumption baseline.
