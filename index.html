Questions

What is the “average*”?

It is the official combined fuel (or energy) consumption rating of your exact model, year and version, published by Natural Resources Canada. CruisE compares your full-tank fill-ups over the last 12 months with that rating.

Does CruisE work for electric cars?

Yes. Log charging sessions in kWh, at home or on public chargers, and compare with the official energy rating.

Do I need to give my location?

No. Location is only used if you choose “Start from my location” in the trip planner, and you can turn it off at any time.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal. You keep Premium until the end of the period.

Is CruisE available in French?

Oui ! The whole app is available in English and French — switch anytime in Account.
