Average* — where it comes from

The “average*” is the official combined fuel consumption rating (or energy consumption rating for electric vehicles) of your exact model, year and version, as published by Natural Resources Canada in its Fuel Consumption Ratings. These ratings come from standardized laboratory tests meant to reflect typical driving; your real consumption depends on driving style, speed, weather, winter tires, load and maintenance.
